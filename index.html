Services

Three focused offerings. If your need falls outside them, ask anyway and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

For businesses

Websites: build, host, maintain

A professional site for your business, set up so that you own it and keep running it.

  • Brochure and service-business sites, landing pages, contact and booking forms
  • Domain, DNS and custom business email setup
  • Basic search visibility, mobile layout and page speed
  • Monthly care plans: updates, backups, monitoring and a set number of content changes

Your domain, hosting and email accounts stay in your name. I'm added as an administrator.

For homeowners

Smart home planning and setup guidance

Help choosing and configuring devices so your home runs the way you want, without the trial and error.

  • Device recommendations matched to your home and budget
  • Hub, app and automation setup (Home Assistant and mainstream platforms)
  • Walkthroughs if you'd like to do the install yourself
  • Referrals to licensed electricians for any wiring work

I don't do electrical work. Where a device needs wiring, I'll refer a licensed contractor or guide a homeowner through what's permitted. I may earn a referral commission on some devices, and I'll tell you when I do.

For new businesses

Business tech starter

Already have your LLC or business registration? I'll set up everything your business needs to look professional online, in one package.

  • Domain registration and custom business email
  • A simple website with a contact form
  • Invoicing and payment setup through a hosted checkout such as Stripe
  • Bookkeeping software set up and ready for your accountant

I don't form companies or give legal, tax or accounting advice. I can point you to the right professional for those.
